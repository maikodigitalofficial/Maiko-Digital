FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before starting your project with Maiko Digital.

How long does it take to build my salon website?

Most projects are delivered within 7 days. Starter packages can be ready in as little as 3 days. We'll give you an exact timeline during our discovery call based on your specific needs.

Do I need to pay monthly fees for the website?

No monthly fees from us. You only pay the one-time project fee. You'll need your own domain (about KES 1,500/year) and hosting (we recommend Netlify or Vercel which are free for most sites).

Can clients book appointments directly on the website?

Absolutely. Our Growth and Elite packages include built-in booking systems. Clients can select services, choose time slots, and even pay deposits — all without you answering a single call.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every site we build is optimized for search engines from the ground up. We handle technical SEO, speed optimization, and local SEO so you rank when people search "salon near me" in your area.

What if I don't like the design?

We offer unlimited revisions during the design phase. We don't move to development until you're 100% happy with the mockup. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
